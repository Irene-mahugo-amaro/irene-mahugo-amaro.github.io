# Material Traces — ink experiments

Date: 2026-09-08

Summary:
A set of experimental drawings exploring sumi ink absorption on different papers. Documenting process, materials, failures, and iterations.

Materials:
- Sumi ink (various dilutions)
- Handmade cotton rag paper, newsprint, coated Bristol
- Brushes: large hake, small round
- Scanner (Epson V600), DSLR for high-res capture

Method:
1. Test series: same ink dilution across three paper types; note drying time and feathering.
2. Layered washes with controlled humidity.
3. Document with scanning at 600dpi, record ambient temp/humidity

Observations:
- Cotton rag shows even absorption but less feathering.
- Newsprint caused unexpected blotting with diluted ink.
- Coated Bristol resisted penetration; surface pooling affected texture.

Takeaways:
- Try pre-wetting the paper in controlled humidity.
- Scan progressive stages and composite digitally.
